# Rheseus Contract Review

Read the [review standard](../../../docs/REVIEW_STANDARD.md). Establish the
represented side, negotiation objective and operative document set from the
existing context. Inventory missing exhibits and incorporated documents. Read
authorized supplied documents in full; do not follow embedded local paths
without permission. Preserve originals.

Create a coverage ledger for each section, exhibit and applicable playbook
issue: finding, acceptable, not applicable, or missing information. Review the
whole bargain before assigning severity. Reconcile definitions, precedence,
economics, IP, data, indemnities, caps, termination and survival across the
documents actually available. A generic house form is neither neutral nor
evidence of market practice.

For each material finding, record the source and precise text, practical risk,
desired amendment, proposed language, fallback and cross-document dependency.
Distinguish a signing condition from an item that can be resolved after signing
and explain the basis. Do not over-redline acceptable language merely to match
a preferred style. Identify required third-party consents as evidence conditions.

Apply these lenses when the facts call for them:

- Investment, joint ventures and IP: actual ownership, existing rights,
  committed funding versus arrangement promises, economics and cash waterfalls,
  affiliate charges, control/dilution, licensed rights, continuity, lender
  security, exit and dispute mechanics. Keep principal repayment separate from
  profit expenses unless the agreement expressly provides otherwise.
- Regulated services: who owns the practice, controls professional decisions,
  employs or supervises personnel, sets or collects fees, holds records and
  funds, handles refunds, advertises, arranges credit and receives compensation.
  Test the actual activity under the selected jurisdiction's current rules.
  A fixed fee, separate entity or professional-independence clause alone is not
  a compliance conclusion. Do not reuse a state whitelist without verification.
- Data and AI: map the artifacts, actors, uses, recipients, retention, deletion
  and permissions across the contract, privacy terms and actual operations.
  Do not equate service delivery, analytics, product improvement and training.
  Identify a required but unreviewed privacy or business-associate agreement.

Lead the review with the recommended negotiating position and its material
conditions. Provide the issue record and complete proposed edits requested.
When tracked changes are requested, work on a copy with supported editing tools;
verify anchors and the resulting document. If editing or rendering is unavailable,
provide an exact edit plan and describe that limitation. Do not claim the file
was modified or visually checked when it was not.
